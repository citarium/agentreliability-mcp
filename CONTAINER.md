## What it serves

`fetch-corpus.mjs` downloads this instance's published corpus and
`citarium-mcp` answers from it. The bytes are the deployment's own, so
the container reports the corpus version it actually holds — an image
built last month says last month's version rather than claiming today's.

The corpus is a SNAPSHOT taken when the image was built. The live endpoint
above is the thing itself, and the one a client should point at. The tools
and their schemas are identical either way: they come from the framework,
not from the corpus.
